# Use Redux

[redux.isu.edu][redux-site] is a workbench for hard computer science problems. You pick a
problem, give it an instance, and then:

- **solve** it with one of several algorithms and compare them,
- **verify** a proposed answer,
- **reduce** it to another problem, and
- **visualize** all of it, step by step and side by side.

Everything runs in your browser, with nothing to install and no account needed.

The site also has its own short guided tour. Select the **Getting started** button on the home
page to take it.

## What's on the site

The tabs at the top of every page lead to:

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The workbench, where you do everything on this page |
| **About Us** | The people who built Redux, its publications, and its license |
| **Browse** | Every problem as a card, filtered by type, class, and reductions |
| **Help** | Background reading and links to the API |
| **Contribute** | How to become a Redux contributor |

The theme button at the top right switches between light and dark mode. On a first visit the site
follows your device's setting.
